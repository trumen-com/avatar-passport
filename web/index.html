Capture tips
  • Walk slowly all the way around the car, about 2–3 m away.
  • Keep the whole car in frame; hold the phone steady.
  • Daylight works best; avoid filming straight into the sun.
  • VIN photo: close-up, sharp, without glare or reflections.
  • Try to keep people out of the shot. Plates and faces are blurred before anything is stored.
