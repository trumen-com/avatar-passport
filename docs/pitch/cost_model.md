# Cost model

- **Status:** 2026-10-11. **Nothing has been purchased.** HF PRO is pending the user's decision at the Run 2 decision point (ADR 003).
- **Number rules.** Every number here is one of:
  - copied from a file in this repo, with the path;
  - from a public source already cited in `docs/research/` or `docs/decisions/`;
  - an **ASSUMPTION** in the parameters table of `roi_model.md` ("replace with partner data");
  - derived from the above. The formula is shown, and the result is generated by `scripts/roi_calc.py`.
- **Generated blocks.** Tables between `<!-- BEGIN:x -->` and `<!-- END:x -->` are written by `python scripts/roi_calc.py`. Do not edit them by hand. `--check` exits 1 when they are stale.
- **Labels.**
  - Laptop numbers are "measured on the dev laptop (RTX 5050 / Ryzen 7 260)".
  - HF CPU Basic numbers are a "2-thread pinned proxy, optimistic lower bound" until Phase 4 measures on the Space.
- **Paid services:** per CLAUDE.md section 9, every paid service or API is listed here. None is added without asking the user first.

How to read this table:
- **Different inputs.** The rows do not process the same input. The GPU end-to-end run used 72 frames and no photos. The CPU smoke runs capped the video at 24 frames (`AVTO_MAX_FRAMES=24`; `docs/research/smoke_test_results.md`, Observations 1) and added 9 photos.

- **Capacity.** Inspections per hour = 3600 / seconds per inspection, one inspection at a time.

## 5. Cost per inspection

**Formula**
- cost per inspection = fixed hosting per month / inspections per month + marginal cost per inspection.
- fixed hosting = HF PRO $9/month + CPU Basic $0/hour.
- inspections per month = branches x inspections per branch per day x working days. These are **ASSUMPTIONS**; see the parameters table in `roi_model.md`.

**Marginal cost = $0**, for three reasons:
1. CPU Basic has no hourly charge ([Spaces overview][hf-overview]).
2. The pipeline calls **no paid API**. Roboflow hosted inference is not used (`docs/research/model_candidates.md`).
3. The external services are free public services, each with a usage policy:

| service | used for | terms | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| NHTSA vPIC | VIN decode | FAQ: "free for use by the public"; the API page mentions "automated traffic rate control" | `docs/research/vin_decoding.md` section 6 |
| OpenStreetMap Overpass | nearby body shops | main server: under 10,000 queries and 1 GB per day, divide by 100 for regular use; send a unique User-Agent, wait 30 s after a 429 | `docs/research/phase0_findings_deploy_market.md` section 8 |
| Nominatim | geocoding | at most 1 request per second, cache results | `docs/research/phase0_findings_deploy_market.md` section 8 |
| freetsa.org (RFC 3161) | trusted timestamp (researched; not in `src/` as of this writing) | no SLA, no stated rate limit; asks commercial users to get in touch | `docs/research/phase0_findings_deploy_market.md` section 5 |

A commercial pilot should contact freetsa (or use another timestamp authority) and respect the vPIC and Overpass policies. The app caches results.

How to read this table:
- **Scenarios.** Low, base and high are the pessimistic, base and optimistic **ASSUMPTION** sets in `roi_model.md`. A higher volume means a lower fixed cost per inspection.
- **Throughput.** One Space processes one inspection at a time. "Compute hours per working day" must stay well below the opening hours, or uploads queue.
- **Storage.** It is the measured size of one laptop run's outputs: anonymised frames, masks and result, plus the 3D outputs. What the deploy keeps per inspection may be smaller (for example, no depth file). Not yet decided.
- **Dev laptop cost.** On the laptop the cash cost per inspection is $0. Electricity and hardware depreciation are not costed.
